"Cowpasture" consists of three 3D modeled scenes resembling landscapes of Bath County, VA. When presented, these scenes are interactive and become jittered when sound is detected.

"Instrospectacular" by Arnie Brenn. I animated this visualizer for the album release on 4/20/2025. I used primarily Blender, Photoshop, Premiere, and Reaper.
